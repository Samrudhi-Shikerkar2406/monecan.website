The Founder

A Passion That Became a Profession

It started with a camera and a genuine love for capturing moments that words cannot fully describe. Long before photography became a business, it was a personal obsession — the way a single frame can hold an entire emotion, a whole memory, a lifetime of meaning.

Over time, that passion grew into something more. Weddings, naming ceremonies, birthdays, corporate events — each one a new story waiting to be told through the lens. Today, Mone_can Photography brings that same sincerity and attention to every event across Goa.

The second chapter came from cricket. A lifelong love of the sport led to a simple observation: Goa had only one flood light vendor for its cricket grounds and events. Arohan Rental was built to fill that gap — and to set a new standard for what professional event lighting looks like in the state.

"I did not set out to build businesses. I set out to do things I genuinely cared about. The businesses followed."
